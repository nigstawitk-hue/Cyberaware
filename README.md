# CyberAware
### Learn to Stay Safe in the Digital World

CyberAware is a cybersecurity awareness and education website designed to help students aged 13–18 understand digital safety, recognize online risks, and develop responsible online habits.

## Our Mission
Our mission is to make cybersecurity easier to understand and accessible to young people. Through interactive lessons, quizzes, challenges, and practical examples, students can learn how to protect their information and make safer decisions online.

## Learning Topics
- Introduction to Cybersecurity
- Password Security
- Phishing and Online Scams
- Social Media Privacy
- Digital Footprints
- Safe Browsing
- Personal Data Protection
- Cyberbullying Awareness
- Online Safety and Responsible Technology Use

## Learning Approach
CyberAware will use:
- Interactive lessons
- Knowledge quizzes
- Student challenges
- Real-world scenarios
- Practical safety tips
